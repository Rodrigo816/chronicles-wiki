  <AccordionItem header="How do I perform rituals? How do they work?">
    The ritual system is explained in the quest book, under the <span className="bold-primary">Alshanex's Familiars</span> chapter.

    ---

    Hidden deep within the vast oceans lies the mysterious <span className="bold-primary">Origin Island</span>, a forgotten land that holds the ancient secrets behind the existence of familiars.

    Its only residents are tiny lost souls — early, incomplete forms of familiars that never reached their final evolution.

    These delicate beings can be <span className="bold-primary">captured using Empty Bottles</span> and are essential for performing powerful **rituals** on the island.

    :::info How rituals work
    Rituals are carried out at the <span className="bold-primary">heart of Origin Island</span>, using the pedestals found there.

    They require specific crafting recipes, and  
    **➡️ The order you place the items matters!**
    :::

    To find the island, you’ll first need a <span className="bold-primary">Sniffer</span>. Occasionally, Sniffers will dig up rare <span className="bold-primary">Crystal Berries</span>.  
    Feeding these to your <span className="bold-primary">tamed familiars</span> unlocks memories of their origins, which solidify into <span className="bold-primary">Memory Fragments</span>.

    These fragments act like <span className="bold-primary">Eyes of Ender</span>, guiding you toward the hidden <span className="bold-primary">Origin Island</span>.
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem header="How do I generate FE (Forge Energy) ?">
  To generate FE, you need to craft an <span className="bold-primary">Alternator</span> from the *Craft & Additions* mod.

  Once placed, it will convert <span className="bold-primary">rotational power</span> from Create into <span className="bold-primary">Forge Energy</span>.

  You can learn more about this setup in the quest book, under the  
  <span className="bold-primary">Create – Electricity</span> chapter.
  </AccordionItem>  
  <AccordionItem header="Why can't I craft a specific spell?">
  Some spells are <span className="bold-primary">exclusive to the Market</span> and <span className="bold-primary">cannot be crafted or found in loot</span>.

  You can view the full list of exclusive spells in the quest book under:  
  <span className="bold-primary">Irons and Spellbooks → _Spells in Market 101</span>
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem header="Is there a way to extract an enchantment from equipment?">
    Yes! You’ll need an <span className="bold-primary">Enchanting Template</span>.

    Simply place your <span className="bold-primary">enchanted item</span> and an <span className="bold-primary">Enchanting Template</span> into the <span className="bold-primary">Blaze Forge</span>.  
    It will remove <span className="bold-primary">one random enchantment</span> from the item and transfer it to the template.

    Each template can hold <span className="bold-primary">only one enchantment</span>, so you may need to repeat the process multiple times until you extract the one you want.

    If your enchantment level is too high, you might need to use <span className="bold-primary">Super Enchanting</span> to reapply it later.

    You can learn more about this in the quest book, under the  
    <span className="bold-primary">Create – Enchantment Industry</span> chapter.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem header="How do I pick up spawners?">
    You’ll need a <span className="bold-primary">Pickaxe with Silk Touch</span> to mine and collect spawners.
  </AccordionItem>  
  <AccordionItem header="How do I change the World Tier?">
    You'll remain in <span className="bold-primary">Haven Tier</span> until you defeat the Ender Dragon.

    > “But how do I get Apotheosis gear if there are no Invasions?”

    Good question! You’ll earn <span className="bold-primary">Apotheosis gear</span> by defeating bosses — many of them drop <span className="bold-primary">Dust</span> and <span className="bold-primary">Gems</span>, which are required to progress.

    Follow the <span className="bold-primary">The Endventure Chapter</span> boss progression in the quest book.

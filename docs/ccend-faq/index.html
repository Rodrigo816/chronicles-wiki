Crystal Shards are obtained through crafting rituals.

The ritual system is explained in the quest book, under the Alshanex's Familiars chapter.

Hidden deep within the vast oceans lies the mysterious Origin Island, a forgotten land that holds the ancient secrets behind the existence of familiars.

Its only residents are tiny lost souls — early, incomplete forms of familiars that never reached their final evolution.

These delicate beings can be captured using Empty Bottles and are essential for performing powerful rituals on the island.

How rituals work

Rituals are carried out at the heart of Origin Island, using the pedestals found there.

They require specific crafting recipes, and
➡️ The order you place the items matters!

To find the island, you’ll first need a Sniffer. Occasionally, Sniffers will dig up rare Crystal Berries.
Feeding these to your tamed familiars unlocks memories of their origins, which solidify into Memory Fragments.

These fragments act like Eyes of Ender, guiding you toward the hidden Origin Island.

To generate FE, you need to either craft an Alternator from the Craft & Additions mod or adventure into a more advanced and more efficient method with the Generator Coil from New Age.

Either one will convert rotational power from Create into Forge Energy. You can learn more about this setup in the quest book, under the Create – Electricity chapter.

Some spells are exclusive to the Market and cannot be crafted or found in loot.

Simply place your enchanted item and an Enchanting Template into the Blaze Forge.
It will remove one random enchantment from the item and transfer it to the template.

Each template can hold only one enchantment, so you may need to repeat the process multiple times until you extract the one you want.

If your enchantment level is too high, you might need to use Super Enchanting to reapply it later.

“But how do I get Apotheosis gear if there are no Invasions?”

Good question! You’ll earn Apotheosis gear by defeating bosses — many of them drop Dust and Gems, which are required to progress.

Follow the The Endventure Chapter boss progression in the quest book.

Apotheosis bookshelves have been disabled in favor of the Create Enchantment Industry progression system.

Enchanting progression is now handled through Create’s automation-based setup instead of bookshelf power scaling.
